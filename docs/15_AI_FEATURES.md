# JobAllocate AI Assistant & Features Specification

> **Document Version:** 1.0.0

---

## 1. AI System Scope
1. **AI Chat Coach (`AiChatScreen`)**: Interactive chat helper for career advice, resume tips, and interview prep (`POST /api/v1/ai/chat`).
2. **AI Resume Text Assistant**: Refines summary bullet points in Resume Studio (`POST /api/v1/job-seeker/resume/ai-assist`).
3. **AI Career Coach (`SeekerAiCoachScreen`)**: `POST /api/v1/job-seeker/career/ai-coach`.
